Peanut butter cookies

Makes 14 cookies

Ingredients
  • 3/4 cup (200g) peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup (92g) sugar
  • 4.5 Tbsp (68g) plant milk
  • 3/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 Tbsp vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup (90g) all-purpose flour
Instructions
  1. Preheat the oven to 350F.
  2. In a large bowl, stir together the peanut butter, sugar, plant milk, and baking soda until well combined.
  3. Add in the flour. Stir until just combined.
  4. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Divide the dough into cookies. Roll each cookie into a ball.
  5. Use a fork to press a criss-cross pattern into each cookie.
  6. Bake for 12 minutes.
